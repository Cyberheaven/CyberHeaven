At **CyberHeaven University** , we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our website, educational resources, community platforms, and services.

## 1\. Who We Are

CyberHeaven University is an educational organization focused on developing cybersecurity professionals through education, training, problem-solving, communication, and practical learning.

Our objective is to help preserve and develop genuine knowledge and independent problem-solving abilities in the next generation of cyberdefenders.

CyberHeaven University currently operates and may provide services through platforms including:

  * Our website
  * Educational and informational content
  * Community platforms such as Discord
  * Learning management systems such as Moodle
  * Online courses and educational programs
  * Contact and communication forms
  * Newsletter and email communications
  * Other services and platforms we may introduce in the future

For the purposes of this Privacy Policy, these services are collectively referred to as the **“Services.”**

## 4\. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies.

Cookies are small files stored on your device that allow a website to remember information about your visit.

We may use cookies or similar technologies for purposes such as:

  * Keeping the website functioning properly
  * Maintaining authentication or session information
  * Remembering preferences
  * Improving website functionality
  * Understanding how visitors interact with the website
  * Protecting forms and services from abuse
  * Maintaining website security

Some cookies may be placed by third-party services that provide functionality for our website.

You can generally control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of parts of the website.
